Analyze the following text for its pacing, sentence structure, and rhythmic flow. Focus on identifying areas where the narrative speed feels unintentional or where repetitive structures create a monotonous reading experience. Focus on these categories:

- Sentence length monotony — consecutive sentences of similar length, particularly a long string of medium-length sentences. Flag areas where a lack of variation in word count dampens the dynamic energy of the prose.

- Structural repetition — recurring syntactical patterns, such as starting multiple consecutive sentences with the same pronoun and verb, or overusing the same grammatical constructions like trailing participial phrases.

- Pacing mismatches — instances where the sentence structure contradicts the narrative tension. Flag action scenes bogged down by complex, multi-clause sentences, or slow, introspective moments rushed by abrupt, staccato phrasing.

- Overused transitions and breathers — sentences weighed down by unnecessary conjunctions, adverbial transitions, or filler words that interrupt the natural flow and drag the pacing.

- Missing rhythmic emphasis — moments of high impact or revelation buried within the middle of a long paragraph or a convoluted sentence, rather than isolated structurally for punch and dramatic effect.

Do not flag deliberate repetition used for thematic resonance, intentional staccato rhythms meant to convey panic or exhaustion, or sprawling sentences explicitly crafted to reflect a character's spiraling thoughts. If a passage is borderline, flag it but note that it may be a conscious craft choice.

For each issue found, provide a brief explanation of why the current structure hinders the pacing or rhythm, and offer a rewritten alternative(s). Match the voice, tense, and point of view of the original.
